Elior Watches: CRO Mockups

Before and after concepts for the cart drawer and the checkout, built to lift the two stages where the funnel leaks: add to cart to checkout at 25.7 percent, and checkout completion at 39.3 percent. These are repositioning and clarity changes, not a rebuild. Shipping protection stays in the cart given its strong take rate.

Current, where attention or trust leaks Proposed change
Fix One

The Cart Drawer

The drawer is well stocked. The issue is that several decisions sit above the checkout button, so the eye reaches Secure Checkout last. We make checkout the clear primary action, add a one-tap express row, keep the high-performing shipping protection, move the lower-take case bump to a post-purchase offer, and simplify the duplicated savings lines.

Fix Two

The Checkout

Checkout completion at 39.3 percent is the single highest-value fix on the store. PayPal already shows as express in the US. The changes here are adding Klarna as express, giving the phone field a reason, and adding a trust row by the total so reassurance lands at the moment of payment.
